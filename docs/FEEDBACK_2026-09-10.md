## The one thing that changes the plan

**Frank: the comprehension quiz is the "golden goose" (24:40).** The advice is
to narrow the MVP to that single feature - extract discharge instructions,
generate questions, measure understanding - validate it, and publish a
simplified build to the app stores for market feedback.

This is a strategic redirection, not an action item, and it sits alongside two
earlier instructions rather than replacing them:

- LOF, 26 Aug: *"Milestone 3 is technical and clinical VALIDATION, not new
  features. Avoid feature overload."* The quiz focus is the same instruction
  with a target attached.
- Liebovitz's plan puts weeks 11-13 on the teach-back study in 10-15 lay
  readers. That study IS the quiz validation Frank is asking for.

**The three now point the same way**, which is worth saying explicitly: the
teach-back study is no longer one deliverable among many. It is the critical
path.

**John (29:49)** made the sharpest version of the point: the six-grade reading
level metric misses retention, and comprehension testing is what the product
has that nothing else does. That is the same distinction Liebovitz drew when
he said readability formulas do not measure comprehension - independently
arrived at by two reviewers.

## Recorded with a caveat

**"TerraCare"** is the transcript's spelling. `docs/ISSUES_AND_IDEAS.md`
records the dependent-profile reference as **TheraCareAI**. These are probably
the same company and the name should be confirmed before it goes into any
written plan - a partner's name spelled two ways in two documents is the kind
of error that is embarrassing to discover late.

**"reduces readmissions"** appears throughout the summary as a product
benefit. It is not measured. Liebovitz's review item 2.4 asks specifically to
*"tie the readmission claim to a specific measurable outcome rather than to
the general association"*, and nothing in this project measures readmission.
The comprehension delta is what the quiz measures, and it is a real number.
The readmission link is a hypothesis and must be stated as one.
