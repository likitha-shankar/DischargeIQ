# Task 2.7 - Issues and Ideas List ✅

**Deliverable:** a maintained list of blockers and parked ideas, kept per
John's guidance.

**Artifact:** `docs/ISSUES_AND_IDEAS.md`

**Format:** open issues and blockers first, then ideas parked for later so
nothing is lost. Newest first within each section. For the developer's own
clarity; available at reviews on request.

## Why it earns its place

It is the only file that records things that are WRONG and not yet fixed.
Every other document in `docs/` describes what works. Without this one, a
known limitation like the process-local session store (I-8) or the vacuous
adversarial audit (I-5) would exist only in someone's memory, and would be
discovered by a reviewer rather than disclosed to them.

Resolved entries stay in place with their resolution recorded (see I-3,
closed by the add-to-calendar button) rather than being deleted, so the list
also shows what was cleared and how.
