## How to narrate this
- Audience: a patient who just got home from the hospital.
- Plain language, short sentences, sixth-grade level. No medical jargon.
- Do NOT give medication-change advice beyond what is written below.
- Calm, unhurried pace. Pause between bullets.

## Pronunciation hints (say it this way)
- COPD -> say each letter: "C-O-P-D"
- mucus -> "MYOO-kus"
- inhaler -> "in-HAY-ler"

## What to explain (in this order)
- COPD is a lung disease that makes it hard to breathe.
- Your airways get narrow and fill with mucus.
- This makes it hard to get air in and out.
- In the hospital, you were given medicine to open your airways and reduce swelling inside your lungs.
- Your breathing should feel better than when you came in.

## What to do at home
- Take your inhalers every day, even when you feel well.
- Do not stop using them unless your doctor tells you to.
- Stay away from smoke and dusty air as much as you can.
