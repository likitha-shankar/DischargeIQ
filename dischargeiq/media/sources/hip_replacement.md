## How to narrate this
- Audience: a patient who just got home from the hospital.
- Plain language, short sentences, sixth-grade level. No medical jargon.
- Read the three rules slowly, one at a time. They are the safety core.
- Calm, unhurried pace. Pause between bullets.

## Pronunciation hints (say it this way)
- hip joint -> plain, "hip joint"
- (no hard drug names in this one)

## What to explain (in this order)
- Your doctor took out your old hip joint and put in a new one.
- The new joint is made of metal and plastic.
- It needs time to set in place.

## The three rules - for six weeks (read slowly, one at a time)
- Do not cross your legs.
- Do not lean forward past your waist.
- Do not turn your foot inward.
- These rules keep the new joint from slipping out.

## What to expect
- Your care team will say when you can walk with help.
- Light activity is okay after six weeks.
- Full healing takes three to six months.
